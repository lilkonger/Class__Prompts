## 1. Customer Background

Northstar Financial Services operates **14 regional offices** and relies on cloud backup services to protect:

- Financial reporting
- Customer records
- Internal business applications

---

## 2. Reported Issue

The customer reports intermittent backup failures affecting several business-critical systems over the past three days.

### Key Findings

- Approximately **35% of scheduled backup jobs have failed**.
- Failures primarily occur during overnight processing.
- **Three critical applications** are affected.
- The last confirmed successful backup for one application was **November 17**.
- No confirmed data loss has been reported.

---

## 3. Customer Concerns

The customer has expressed concerns about:

- Potential inability to recover systems during an outage.
- Possible impact on internal recovery objectives.
- Uncertainty about the reliability of recent backups.
- Lack of clear communication regarding resolution timelines.

---

## 4. Investigation Notes

The support team has identified the following potential contributing factors:

1. **Network Connectivity**
   - Intermittent connectivity between the backup environment and cloud storage.

2. **Authentication**
   - Possible authentication token expiration.

3. **Infrastructure Changes**
   - Increased backup workload following a recent infrastructure update.

4. **Diagnostic Information**
   - Incomplete diagnostic logs for two failed jobs.

> **Important:** The root cause has not been confirmed.
